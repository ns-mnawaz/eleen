Eleen — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Eleen collects no personal data. Period.

Eleen is a parental screen-time manager for Android TV that works entirely on your home network. The app on your TV and the app on your phone talk to each other directly over your local Wi-Fi. No information is ever sent over the internet, to us, or to anyone else.

What we collect

Nothing. Eleen has no accounts, no analytics, no tracking, no ads, and no third-party SDKs that collect data. We do not see, store, or process any information about you, your children, or your devices.

What stays on your devices

Profile names, time allowances, your parent PIN, and a pairing token are stored locally on the TV and phone you pair — never transmitted beyond your local network. Uninstalling the app deletes this data.

Permissions

  • Camera (phone only): used solely to scan the pairing QR code shown by the TV. Images are processed on-device and never stored or transmitted.
  • Local network: used solely for communication between Eleen on your TV and Eleen on your phone on the same Wi-Fi.
  • Display over other apps (TV only): used to show the time-limit screen when TV time runs out.

Children

Eleen is operated by parents. It does not collect, request, or store any information from or about children. Profile names are whatever the parent chooses and remain on your own devices.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the update will appear here and in the app's Play Store listing before any change takes effect.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue on the project's GitHub repository.
