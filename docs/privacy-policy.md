# Eleen Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-10-29

Eleen is a parental screen-time app for Android TV and Android phones.
This policy explains how the app handles data.

## Summary

**Eleen collects no personal data and shares no data with anyone.**

## Details

- **No data collection.** Eleen does not collect, transmit, or analyze any
  personal or sensitive user data. There are no analytics, no advertising
  SDKs, no crash reporters, and no third-party services of any kind.
- **All sync stays on your local network.** The TV app and the parent's
  phone app communicate directly with each other over your home Wi-Fi /
  local network (LAN). Profile names, daily allowances, remaining screen
  time, and usage history are stored only on your own devices and
  exchanged only between them. No remote server is involved, and none of
  this information ever leaves your household network.
- **Storage is local only.** All data (profiles, sessions, settings) is
  stored in a local database on your TV and phone. Deleting the app
  deletes this data.
- **Permissions.** Eleen requests only the permissions needed to enforce
  screen time: "Display over other apps" on the TV (to show countdowns and
  the time's-up lock over running apps), camera access on the phone (only
  to scan the TV's pairing QR code, in the app, on your device), and local
  network access (TV↔phone pairing and sync). These permissions are never
  used to collect data.
- **Children.** Eleen is aimed at parents, who configure and control it.
  Since no data is collected, no data about children is collected either.
